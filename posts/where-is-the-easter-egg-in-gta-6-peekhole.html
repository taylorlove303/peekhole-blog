GTA 6 Easter eggs

Where Is the Easter Egg in GTA 6? | Peekhole

November 20, 2026

Short answer: at the very top of the Bluegrass Radio Mast, the tallest red-and-white radio tower in Vice-Dale County. There's a bird's nest up there holding painted Easter eggs. Yes, Rockstar made a literal Easter egg.

How do you get it?

Climb the tower all the way to the top. Player xGarbett, who first posted it, said the climb took about seven minutes, so bring patience. Grab an egg from the nest and throw it off the ledge.

What happens when you throw it?

A short cutscene plays. Leonida Players says the egg smashes through the windshield of a yellow convertible far below. GameSpot says it lands on a man who sounds like a sleazy real estate agent or lawyer. Either way, it's a perfect shot.

What other secrets have players found?

Plenty, and it's only day two. GTAVIMaps lists a UFO abduction east of Port Gellhorn at night that unlocks a hidden trophy called "The Truth Is Out There," and a Bigfoot sighting near Cottonmouth Lake. It also flags a giant crab cutscene that nobody knows how to trigger yet, so treat that one as unsolved.
