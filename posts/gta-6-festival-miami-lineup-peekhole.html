Vice City nightlife, IRL

Who Is Playing the GTA 6 Festival in Miami? | Peekhole

November 14, 2026

Short answer: Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival is on November 21, 2026, at Mana Wynwood in Miami, two days after the game launches. The lineup includes Travis Scott, Cardi B, Justice, Metro Boomin, Kodak Black, and more.

The full lineup

  • Travis Scott, Don Toliver, Kodak Black, and Sofaygo
  • Cardi B
  • Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso
  • Justice (DJ set)
  • Metro Boomin
  • Nick León b2b DJ Python
  • Prospa b2b Skream
  • Rauw & Friends
  • Suero
  • Tainy
  • The Dare b2b Fcukers (DJ set)

Can I still get tickets?

Probably not. Tickets went through a lottery on DICE that closed November 10. Winners could buy up to two tickets on November 11, and Polygon reports the codes can't be sold or traded.

Why a festival?

It's a launch party for the game's music. Rockstar and PlayStation are hosting it, with two stages. GTA 6: The Album, 34 original tracks, drops November 19 with the game, and Justice's Original Score follows on December 11.

So the real-life version of Vice City nightlife lands in actual Miami, neon and all. Everyone else gets to party in Leonida from the couch.
