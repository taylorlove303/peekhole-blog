Is GTA 6 Buggy? Launch Bugs and Fixes | Peekhole

November 19, 2026

Short answer: a little. GTA 6 launched on November 19 to rave reviews, but players are posting clips of small bugs, a few crashes, and PlayStation sign-in errors. As of the morning of November 19, Rockstar hadn't commented on them or posted patch notes.

What bugs are people seeing?

  • Falling through the map: the big one. Lucia can drop through the floor in the prologue. She usually pops back up on her own.
  • Clipping: Jason and a police officer sinking into a couch, or Jason boarding a plane without the door animation in the Gone Fishing mission.
  • Wild physics: cars launching into the air after crashes.
  • Crashes and stuck missions: some players report crashes in the prologue, lost progress, and an early Lucia mission that won't finish after leaving a bank.
  • PSN sign-in errors: Moneycontrol says Downdetector logged over 1,000 reports in a few hours during the launch rush.

How do you fix them?

Simple stuff first. If you get stuck, reload your last save. If Lucia doesn't respawn or a mission won't move on, restart the game and replay the mission. Keep a few manual saves so a crash can't eat hours of play. Report anything serious to Rockstar Support.

Is a patch coming?

Probably, but nothing is official. A post on X claiming a fix is set for November 25 is unverified, so treat it as rumor. Most outlets say these bugs are annoying, not game-breaking.
