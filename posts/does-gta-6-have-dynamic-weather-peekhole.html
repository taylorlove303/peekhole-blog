Does GTA 6 Have Dynamic Weather? | Peekhole

November 17, 2026

Short answer: Yes. Rockstar says it rebuilt GTA 6's weather to be "more localized, persistent, and above all dynamic," so different parts of Leonida can have different weather at the same time.

Where did this come from?

On November 16, The Weather Channel posted an in-character forecast for Leonida on X, with six new screenshots Rockstar gave it as an exclusive. The quote comes from Owen Shepherd, Rockstar's VP of Art for light and rendering. He said Florida's unpredictable climate pushed the studio to "completely reimagine" its weather, from clear blue skies to towering storm clouds.

What did the forecast show?

  • The Keys: heavy rain at night outside a marina.
  • Pelican Valley: a storm over the swamp, with an alligator half hidden in the water.
  • South Ocean Beach: sea fog swallowing Vice City's beachfront towers.
  • The Rusty Anchor: a tropical storm and lightning over a lit pier.
  • Gambit Bay: morning mist "ahead of hunting season."
  • Key Lento: rain and wind on the commute.

Does weather change gameplay?

Not confirmed. GameSpot notes the screenshots do not show how weather affects driving, missions, or anything else. For now, it is confirmed as a big visual and world feature. We find out the rest on November 19.
