GTA 6 Ocean Drive Clip Fools Miami | Peekhole

November 12, 2026

Here at Peekhole, Taylor Love 303 knows a good tease when she sees one, and the GTA 6 Ocean Drive clip is a top-tier tease. It is about 20 seconds of shaky, phone-style video showing wrapped supercars parked along a sunny Vice City strip, and plenty of people thought it was shot in real Miami.

The video went up on November 11 from the Miami Instagram account Only In Dade. Rockstar then shared it to its own Instagram Story and liked the original post, which is why outlets like Kotaku and Polygon treat it as real in-game footage. Rockstar has not made a formal statement about it.

The fun twist: the clip copies a real October promo, when Rockstar and West Coast Customs drove GTA 6 cars around Miami. So the game is now recreating a real event that was staged to promote the game. Peak hall-of-mirrors energy.

Some fans think the two people filming are Jason and Lucia. That is a guess, not a fact. The footage is too blurry to confirm it, and a first-person camera angle does not prove the game has a first-person mode.
