GTA 6 behind the scenes

GTA 6 Rob Nelson Interview: Miami Research | Peekhole

November 12, 2026

Peekhole loves a behind-the-curtain peek, and the new GTA 6 Rob Nelson interview in The Guardian is exactly that. Nelson, described by The Guardian as one of the game's lead developers, walked games editor Keza MacDonald through Vice City at Rockstar North in Edinburgh.

The big reveal is how far back the homework goes. The Guardian says Rockstar North developers started visiting Miami for research in 2015. Reports on the piece mention nightclubs, a racetrack, a police ride-along in South Beach, and bars in the Florida Keys, with the team going “semi-undercover.”

Nelson seemed proudest of the small stuff. In the demo, Jason eats a hot dog, juggles a donut and a beer at a gas station, and tucks a six-pack under his arm when he starts to run.

Asked whether GTA could ever leave the US, Nelson said, “Never say never, but there's still so much to do in America.” That is not an announcement of anything, just a door left open. A longer Guardian feature is expected in its Saturday magazine.
