How Do You Unlock The Vault Radio in GTA 6? | Peekhole

November 27, 2026

Short answer: Go to The Vault nightclub in Vice City, head up the stairs inside, and scan the club's poster with the WAiNK app on your phone. The Vault, an EDM station, then shows up in your radio list.

Where is The Vault nightclub?

It sits in central Vice City, between Crosstown and Downtown. Look for it across the street from NINE1NINE, the strip club you visit for a Chapter 1 story mission. VGC says it has its own map icon, so it's hard to miss.

Which poster do I scan?

  1. Walk in and go up the first flight of stairs.
  2. Find the poster with a gas mask covered in flowers. Shacknews puts it on the wall at the top of the stairs, next to the ATM.
  3. Open your phone, pick WAiNK, aim at the poster and scan it.

The station plays the kind of club music you hear inside. Shacknews says you can play it in cars, on headphones or through a standalone speaker.

Are there other secret stations?

Yes. VGC says Kodak Black's The Sniper of LaPerle unlocks after you scan four posters: three in LaPerle (Drip Drops, Twa Ponya Tattoo and Aunt Zola's Soul Food) and one upstairs in the Jack of Hearts strip club's recording booth. That last one needs Chapter 3.
