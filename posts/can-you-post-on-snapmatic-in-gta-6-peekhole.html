Can You Post on Snapmatic in GTA 6? | Peekhole

November 27, 2026

Short answer: No. Snapmatic, GTA 6's take on Instagram, is read-only. Jason and Lucia can scroll the feed, follow accounts, like posts and read comments, but they can't upload anything of their own.

Why can't you post?

Rockstar explained it before launch. In IGN's full interview with Rockstar North's Rob Nelson, as quoted by Insider Gaming, he said you can't post "because you're a couple on the run. You just lurk." Fair enough. Selfies aren't great for a pair of wanted felons.

So what is Snapmatic good for?

  • Doomscrolling. GameSpot describes a feed of Leonida locals posting about their lives, plus plenty of memes and "brainrot."
  • Finding stuff to do. KeenGamer says some posts let you mark the pictured spot on your map, and some lead to side activities. A marked spot isn't always a mission, though.
  • Story flavor. The feed reacts to things happening around Leonida as the story moves on.

Where do my photos go, then?

You can still snap selfies with the Camera app, and they're saved in the Gallery app. They just never hit the feed.

Is Lifeinvader in GTA 6?

Not as an app. Rockstar's old Facebook parody doesn't appear on GameSpot's list of phone apps. Snapmatic handles the social feed, and texts go through WhatUp.
