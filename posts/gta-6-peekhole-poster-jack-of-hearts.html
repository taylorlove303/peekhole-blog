What Is the GTA 6 Peekhole Poster? | Peekhole

November 13, 2026

Short answer: the GTA 6 Peekhole poster is a "Make It Rain Mondays" ad outside the Jack of Hearts strip club, seen at about the 17:31 mark of Rockstar's video Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look. It shows the Peekhole name, a logo, and a QR code. That is the confirmed part. Everything else is fan reading.

Where did it show up?

The Extended Look came out on August 27, 2026. Fans freeze-framed the club scene, an X fan account shared the find, and PlayStation LifeStyle reported it on October 23. It is not a leak or a datamine. It is sitting in plain sight in official footage.

Does the Peekhole QR code work?

Sort of. Reports from GTA Wiki and PlayStation LifeStyle say scanning it takes you to Rockstar's official GTA VI website, not to any Peekhole page. Rockstar controls that link, so it could change.

Is Peekhole an OnlyFans parody?

That is how fans and GTA Wiki read it. The logo looks a lot like the OnlyFans logo from 2016 to 2021, the name is a cheeky peeping joke, and it is hanging outside a strip club. Rockstar has a history here too: GTA V poked fun at Facebook with Lifeinvader and at Twitter with Bleeter. But Rockstar has not said a word about what Peekhole is.

Can you use Peekhole in the game?

Unknown. No source confirms a Peekhole app, website, or mission. Right now it is set dressing on one poster. We will all find out after GTA 6 launches on November 19.
