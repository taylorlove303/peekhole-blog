GTA 6 news check

Is There a GTA 6 Patch on November 25? | Peekhole

November 22, 2026

Short answer: no. A viral post says Rockstar apologized for lag and set a big fix for November 25, but Rockstar has announced no such patch. As of November 22, GTA 6 is still on version 01.002.001.

Where did the rumor come from?

The post started spreading on X around November 20. GTA6BUGS checked and found it links to no source. A reader-added note on X also says Rockstar made no such announcement. Rockstar's Newswire and Support pages show no apology, no patch date, and no patch notes.

What updates are out so far?

Two, both before launch: 01.002.000 on November 13 and 01.002.001 on November 16. Rockstar didn't publish notes for either. Nothing new has come out since the November 19 launch.

What crashes are people hitting?

The big one is error CE-108255-1 on PS5 and PS5 Pro. Xbox players report crashes to the dashboard and lost progress. GTA6Walk's tracker says most start-up problems clear up once you install PS5 system software 14.23 and game update 01.002.001. Until a real fix lands, save manually and often.

Will there be a patch?

Very likely, since Rockstar usually patches big launches in the first few weeks. But no date is official. Real patch notes show up on Rockstar Support and the Newswire first, so trust those over any random post.
