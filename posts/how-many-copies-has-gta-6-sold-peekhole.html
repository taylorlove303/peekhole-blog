How Many Copies Has GTA 6 Sold? | Peekhole

November 19, 2026

Short answer: about 17 million, but that's an estimate. Market research firm Sensor Tower says GTA 6 sold roughly 17.07 million digital copies and made about $1.65 billion through launch day, November 18. Take-Two hasn't released an official number yet.

How does that compare to GTA V?

It's roughly double in dollars. GTA V made about $800 million in its first 24 hours back in 2013, from around 11.2 million copies, according to a breakdown by The World Data. Keep in mind the GTA 6 figure counts pre-orders and only digital stores, not discs or boxes.

Which console is selling more?

PlayStation, by a lot. Sensor Tower puts PS5 at about 75% of sales, with Xbox Series X|S at the other 25%. There's no PC version yet. That's planned for 2027.

What happens next?

Wall Street is buzzing. Take-Two shares rose about 6% on November 18. Wells Fargo now expects 65 million copies by the end of Take-Two's fiscal year in March. Analyst firm Omdia thinks the game could bring in $3.2 billion by the end of 2026. Those are forecasts, not results, so watch for Take-Two's own numbers.
