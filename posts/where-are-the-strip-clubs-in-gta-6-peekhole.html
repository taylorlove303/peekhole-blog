Where Are the Strip Clubs in GTA 6? | Peekhole

November 21, 2026

Short answer: there are three so far. Polygon lists Delights Cabaret in Port Gellhorn, NINE1NINE in Downtown Vice City, and Jack of Hearts in Vice City on Mirimar Drive.

Where is each one?

Delights Cabaret is a run-down spot near the I-404 and Glenwood Ave, just southeast of Dunk's trailer. You can watch, but there are no private dances here. NINE1NINE is the high-end one, on NE 7th Street right next to The Vault nightclub. Jack of Hearts is a hip-hop themed club in a small shopping area northwest of the three tall towers by the bridge.

How much is a private dance?

Polygon says $300 at NINE1NINE and $1,000 at Jack of Hearts, plus tips. It's cash only, so hit one of the club ATMs first. Both Jason and Lucia can pay for one.

When can you go?

Delights Cabaret and NINE1NINE are open from Chapter 1. Jack of Hearts unlocks at the start of Chapter 3.

Will it hurt Jason and Lucia's relationship?

Not by itself. Your partner may text asking where you are. Polygon says answering honestly and still spending time together keeps things fine. Ignoring texts is what really hurts.

Wait, Jack of Hearts sounds familiar

It should. It's the club on the pre-launch poster that mentions Peekhole. So far Peekhole has only been seen as a name on that poster, not as a working app in the game.
