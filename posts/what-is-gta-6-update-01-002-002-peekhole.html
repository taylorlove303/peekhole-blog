What Is GTA 6 Update 01.002.002? | Peekhole

November 23, 2026

Short answer: GTA 6 update 01.002.002 is the game's first patch since its November 19 launch. It started rolling out on November 23, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Rockstar hasn't said what it changes yet.

How big is it?

Small, thankfully. Dataminer Tex2 put it at 603.8 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox, as reported by VICE. On Xbox it shows up as version 1.0.2.2. GameSpot says you'll need it to keep playing.

Why don't I have it yet?

On PS5 it's going out in waves. VICE reports about a third of players got it first, two-thirds about three hours in, and everyone about six hours after the start. Xbox got it all at once. To check on PS5, highlight GTA 6, press Options, and pick Check for Update.

What does it fix?

Officially, nobody knows. Rockstar hasn't posted patch notes on its Newswire or Support site. GameSpot and Insider Gaming expect bug and stability fixes, and maybe a crackdown on money exploits, but that's a guess. Early player reports on GTA6BUGS say the Chapter 3 bug where Jason stays greyed out is not fixed yet.

Is this the "November 25 patch"?

No. That claim was fake. This one is real, beat that fake date by two days, and came with no apology letter. Ignore any "full patch notes" list that doesn't come from Rockstar itself.
