What Songs Are on the GTA 6 Album? | Peekhole

November 20, 2026

Short answer: 34 brand-new songs. Rockstar released Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album on November 20, the day after the game came out, with new music from Lana Del Rey, Dua Lipa, Playboi Carti, JENNIE, Zara Larsson, Camila Cabello, Rina Sawayama and more.

Which songs are the big ones?

Consequence lists the newly revealed tracks: Lana Del Rey's "Crazy for You," Dua Lipa's "Go Easy," Playboi Carti's "Ahh Yea," JENNIE's "Power Trip," Zara Larsson's "Fast Emotion," Camila Cabello's "Sweet Like Candy," and Rina Sawayama's "TRDWFE." They join songs Rockstar already put out before launch. The mix runs from pop and hip-hop to Latin, R&B, electronic and rock.

Is that all the music in the game?

Not even close. Stereogum points out the album is only a small slice of what plays on the in-game radio. Surprise radio songs include tracks from D'Angelo, Travis Scott, 100 gecs, Central Cee, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard.

Who hosts the radio stations?

Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey and Robyn each have their own station. Stereogum also names Keith Richards and Buddy Guy on a blues station, Gloria and Emilio Estefan on Radio La Ola, and T-Boz and Chilli on Honey FM.

Where can you hear it?

The album is out now on streaming services, and Consequence links a vinyl version too. Fun fact: the tracklist first leaked a week early through vinyl sales.
