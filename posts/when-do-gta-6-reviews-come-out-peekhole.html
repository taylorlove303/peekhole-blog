GTA 6 launch week

When Do GTA 6 Reviews Come Out? | Peekhole

November 17, 2026

Short answer: Most likely Wednesday, November 18, the day before launch. That date is reported, not official. Rockstar has not announced a review embargo, a time, or even that review copies exist.

Where does November 18 come from?

On October 26, the leaker NateTheHate posted on X that Rockstar had started sending review code to outlets and that the "review embargo is November 18th." Insider Gaming says it independently verified the date. Treat it as a strong rumor, not a promise.

What time?

A ResetEra poster called BlackBate put it at roughly 6 to 7 AM Eastern on the 18th. That is about 4 to 5 AM MST. The same post said some sites and YouTube channels may have to wait until the game unlocks at 12:01 AM Eastern on November 19. TheCity.gg points out the reported times land one minute after the New Zealand and US unlocks, which is a neat coincidence, not a confirmation.

Is one day before launch normal for Rockstar?

Yes. GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 reviews also went live a day before release, according to Insert Future.

Any score you see before then?

It's fake. Wait for the real outlets. And if you pre-loaded on PS5, Insert Future notes that starting the download already ended your right to cancel, so the reviews are mostly for bragging or worrying.
