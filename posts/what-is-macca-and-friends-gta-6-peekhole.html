What Is Macca & Friends in GTA 6? | Peekhole

November 14, 2026

Short answer: Macca & Friends is an animated TV show that lives inside GTA 6. It stars Macca, a pink alligator in sunglasses, and the teaser says it is "coming November 19 on WEAZEL," the game's parody TV network. That is launch day.

Who is in it?

Polygon lists the cast as Macca, Pinchie the crab (his landlord, and not really his friend), Chunkee the manatee, a flamingo with no name yet, and a human cop. The plot is pure rent-day panic: Macca needs cash fast or he gets kicked out. GameSpot says the gang seems to dress up as police and sneak into a station to solve that problem. Very wholesome. Very Leonida.

Where did the teaser come from?

The first look went up on November 13 as a DiscussingFilm exclusive on X, and Rockstar's official account reposted it. Rockstar has not written a Newswire post about the show.

What we don't know yet

Nobody outside Rockstar has said how many episodes there are, how long they run, or whether you watch on a safehouse TV, your in-game phone, or both. Polygon and GameSpot both guess it is a real multi-episode show, but that is a guess, not a confirmed feature.

Macca was already on the shelf

Macca is not brand new. The $399.99 Goodtime State Vice City Collection from the Rockstar Store includes a Macca figure and a Chunkee shot glass, and it does not include the game itself.
