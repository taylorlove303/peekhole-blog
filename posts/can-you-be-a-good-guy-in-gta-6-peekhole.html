Can You Be a Good Guy in GTA 6? | Peekhole

November 15, 2026

Short answer: Sort of. Rockstar's Rob Nelson told The Guardian that GTA 6 is the first GTA with ways to encourage empathy, for players who want them. But crime is still the heart of the game, and you won't be punished for playing it the old way.

What did Rockstar actually say?

Asked if there would be some empathy in GTA 6, Nelson said, "I would say so," and added that it comes from the story Rockstar is telling, as quoted by Insider Gaming. He also said your actions will have some effect on how the game plays out.

What does "nice" look like?

Small things, so far. In the demo, Jason asked a stranger if he could pet their dog. People also reacted differently when he interrupted their conversations. Rockstar has not listed the empathy features or said what being kind changes later.

How does this fit the Criminal Profile?

GTA 6 already has a Criminal Profile that tracks what kind of criminal you are, from "Professional" to "Irredeemable," IGN reports. Nelson has said there is no right or wrong way to play, but steady habits can change how the world and story react. He did not say the empathy features are tied to that profile.

So, saint or sinner?

Jason and Lucia are still crooks. The difference is that you get to choose whether they are messy, violent crooks or careful ones who stop to pet the dog.
