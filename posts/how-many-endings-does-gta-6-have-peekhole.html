How Many Endings Does GTA 6 Have? | Peekhole

November 21, 2026

Short answer: at least four. That's how many reviewers and players have confirmed so far, and Rockstar says the story has "a lot more than two" endings. Nobody has an official final count yet.

Do you pick the ending at the last minute?

No. GTA IV and GTA V gave you a big choice right before the finale. GTA 6 doesn't. Clarín explains that your ending builds up across the whole playthrough instead.

What decides your ending?

Four things, according to Clarín's breakdown: big story choices (like sparing or killing certain characters), how close Jason and Lucia are, each one's Criminal Profile, and how much money is in the couple's savings account. GameSpot says the best ending it saw came from a deeply in-love couple with the savings maxed out at two million dollars. A strong relationship without the full savings gave a good, but not best, ending.

Can you replay the finale?

Yes. The game asks before you start the final missions. After the credits, it rewinds to just before that prompt, so you can keep exploring and run the finale again for a different result. Beating the story also unlocks the full completion checklist and the Cheats tab.

Are there really seven or more?

Some players online say they've found more. That's possible, but it isn't confirmed yet, so treat any bigger number as a rumor for now.
