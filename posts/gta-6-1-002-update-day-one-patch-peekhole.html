GTA 6 launch prep

What Is the GTA 6 1.002 Update? | Peekhole

November 14, 2026

Short answer: Version 1.002 is GTA 6's first update. It is about 1GB, it showed up for some players on Friday, November 13, and most fans think it is the day-one patch. Rockstar has not posted any patch notes.

Why didn't I get it yet?

Polygon reports the update looks staggered, meaning it reaches players in batches instead of all at once. If your console hasn't grabbed it, give it time and keep automatic updates on.

Why no patch notes?

The game isn't out until November 19, so detailed notes could spoil things. That's Polygon's reading, not an official Rockstar statement, but it makes sense.

Why patch before launch?

GTA 6 is the most pre-ordered game ever on the PlayStation and Microsoft stores, and the full game is around 212GB. Spreading downloads out early takes pressure off servers before millions of people try to play at midnight.

What to do now

Finish your pre-load, leave room on your drive for at least this 1GB, and check for updates again before launch night. Polygon warns this may not be the last download before November 19. Think of it as getting ready before a big night out: shower, outfit, patch.
