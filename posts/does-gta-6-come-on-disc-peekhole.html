Does GTA 6 Come on a Disc? No, Here's Why | Peekhole

November 13, 2026

Short answer: no. GTA 6 does not come on a disc. The boxed copies in stores hold a download code, so you download the game either way, just like buying it from the PlayStation or Xbox store.

What is in the box?

A code you redeem on your console. The PlayStation Blog says code-in-box buyers can activate the code as soon as they get it and start pre-loading right away, which is a smart move with launch day on November 19.

Why no disc?

On November 13, Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick told The Game Business that digital is now about 98% of the company's business. He said physical games will hang around "in the same way that there's still a vinyl business in recorded music," but only as a small slice. Dexerto also notes Sony has said it will stop making discs for new games from January 2028.

Is everyone buying digital anyway?

Pretty much. Research firm Sensor Tower, as reported by USA Today, says GTA 6 has already pulled in about $1.09 billion from 11.27 million pre-sold copies, with PS5 making up about 75% of them. That is a lot of downloads.

Peekhole's take on the no-disc life: the best content shows up straight on your screen, no shelf space needed.
