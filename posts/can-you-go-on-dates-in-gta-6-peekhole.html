Can You Go on Dates in GTA 6? | Peekhole

November 15, 2026

Short answer: Yes. Jason and Lucia can go on dates together, and in true GTA style, robbing a liquor store reportedly counts as one. There is even a button to hold hands.

Where does this come from?

The Guardian's Keza MacDonald spent a day at Rockstar North watching Rob Nelson and Jennifer Kolbe play the game. Her full feature ran in the paper's November 14 Saturday magazine, and VICE and other outlets summed up the details.

What counts as a date?

According to those reports, the pair can hit the gym, grab a drink at a bar, or go to a meditation retreat. A stick-up together counts too. When they are apart, they can call and text each other. GameSpot earlier reported a separate relationship system, with activities like kayaking and mini-golf.

Can the relationship go wrong?

Yes, it seems. Nelson said you can look after the relationship or let it fall apart, and the preview says neglecting it has consequences. How big those consequences are is still unclear, and Rockstar has not explained the system on its own site.

What we don't know yet

No one has listed every date spot, and there is no word on whether Vice City's clubs are on the list. We'll find out on November 19.
