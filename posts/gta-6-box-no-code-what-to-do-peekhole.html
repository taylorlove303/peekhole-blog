GTA 6 Box Has No Code? What to Do | Peekhole

November 15, 2026

Short answer: If your boxed GTA 6 has a blank grey strip instead of a download code, go back to the store that sold it. Rockstar's own pre-order FAQ sends purchase, refund, and delivery problems to the retailer, and PlayStation's code help page says the same for a damaged or missing code.

What is going wrong?

GTA 6 has no disc. The "physical" copy is a case with a code printed inside it, and boxes started reaching buyers on November 12 so people could pre-load. Polygon and Notebookcheck report that some PS5 and Xbox buyers opened theirs to find a grey strip that looks like a scratch-off but won't scratch. Both outlets say it does not look widespread, and Rockstar has not commented publicly.

What to do, step by step

  • Look twice. The code sits on the inside of the case, where a disc would normally go.
  • Keep everything: the case, the wrapper, the poster, and the receipt.
  • Take clear photos of the blank strip and your receipt.
  • Go back to the store and call it a faulty product, not a change-of-mind return.
  • Bought it on the Rockstar Store? Rockstar's FAQ points you to its payment partner, Xsolla, at help.xsolla.com.

Results so far are mixed. Polygon says Amazon refunded players and some Best Buy stores fixed it while others refused, and GameStop reportedly sent buyers to Rockstar. Two Reddit users say Rockstar Support closed their tickets.

The game unlocks November 19, so you still have a few days to sort it out.
