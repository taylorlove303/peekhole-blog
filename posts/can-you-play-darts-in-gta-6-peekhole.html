Can You Play Darts in GTA 6? | Peekhole

November 23, 2026

Short answer: no. There's no darts in GTA 6, and no bowling either. Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson told VGC the team couldn't make darts good enough in time, so it was left out.

What did Rockstar say?

"The darts one is a contentious one," Nelson told VGC. He said people assume Rockstar has unlimited money and time, but "even we do not." His summary: "It's better not to do it than to do it and have it not be good enough."

Why does it sting?

Darts was in GTA 4, GTA 5 and GTA Online, and bowling was in GTA 4 and its expansions. So plenty of players walked into a Leonida bar expecting a board on the wall. The news is trending again on X this weekend, with some fans asking how a game this big ran out of time.

So what can you do on a night out?

Shoot pool. Nelson says pool "has proper physics; it's a game in its own right." He also points to weightlifting, which copies the real movement instead of button mashing, and basketball, where you can shoot around freely but can't play a full game. Nelson says the thinking started with Red Dead Redemption 2, when Rockstar turned minigames into "activities" that feel like part of the world.

Could darts come later?

Rockstar hasn't said so. Nelson only said that if it were added, it would have to "feel proper." Don't trust anyone promising a darts update until Rockstar announces one.
