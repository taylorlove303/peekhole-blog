GTA 6 launch night

What Time Does GTA 6 Unlock? | Peekhole

November 13, 2026

Short answer: GTA 6 unlocks on November 19, 2026, at midnight local time, rolling around the world country by country. New Zealand goes first at 12:00 AM NZDT, which is 4:00 AM Mountain time on Wednesday, November 18 for us in the US.

PS5

The PlayStation Blog says Sony and Rockstar picked a "rolling midnight launch by country." It starts in New Zealand and moves west as each country hits midnight on November 19.

Xbox and Xbox Cloud Gaming

Xbox Wire says cloud streaming of GTA 6 starts at 1:00 AM ET on November 19. That is how Xbox One owners can play. Polygon reports Xbox Series X|S players also get in from 1:00 AM ET.

The US time zone wrinkle

Here is where sites disagree. Game Rant reports the US stores run on Eastern time, which would mean 9 PM Pacific, 10 PM Mountain, and 11 PM Central on November 18. Other trackers list midnight in each local time zone instead. Rockstar has not posted an official city-by-city unlock table, so check the countdown on your own console's store page.

How to be ready

Pre-load now, keep your console's automatic date and time turned on, and leave it plugged in. Then all you have to do on launch night is stay up. Peekhole knows a thing or two about late nights.
