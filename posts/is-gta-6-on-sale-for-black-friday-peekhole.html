Is GTA 6 on Sale for Black Friday? | Peekhole

November 27, 2026

Short answer: Not officially. Eight days after launch, Rockstar, PlayStation and Xbox still charge $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. A few third-party sellers are trimming the price, though, so you can save around $10 if you shop around.

What are the best GTA 6 Black Friday deals?

  • US, PS5: GamesRadar+ and Benzinga both point to the digital code seller Loaded (formerly CDKeys), which has the PS5 version at $69.99. GamesRadar+ calls it the first discount it has seen on a PS5 copy.
  • US, Xbox: Benzinga lists Loaded at $67.49 for Xbox Series X|S and $85.99 for the Ultimate Edition. It says the promos run through December 1.
  • UK: Radio Times lists PS5 copies at £64.99 at big chains like Currys, Argos and Smyths. Loaded has the Xbox code at about £56, down from £69.99.

What about Amazon and Best Buy?

No luck so far. GamesRadar+ says Amazon is holding at $79.99 on both consoles, and Best Buy was at full price on its last check too.

Is a code from a key site safe?

Check that the region matches your console's account before you buy. GamesRadar+ gives the same tip. Prices can change hour to hour on Black Friday, so check again before you check out.
