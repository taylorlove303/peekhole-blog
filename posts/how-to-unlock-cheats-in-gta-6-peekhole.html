How Do You Unlock Cheats in GTA 6? | Peekhole

November 19, 2026

Short answer: you have to beat the game first. GTA 6 keeps its whole Cheats menu locked until you finish all 91 main story missions, and then each cheat has its own challenge you need to clear before it works.

Where is the cheats menu?

Once the credits roll, open the map, go to the Progress tab, and scroll all the way right. Shacknews says that's where the Cheats list shows up. Hover over a locked cheat to see what it wants from you.

What kind of challenges unlock them?

Think grind, not button mash. GTA Wiki counts 61 cheats in five groups: player, combat, vehicle spawns, environment, and screen filters. A few examples it lists: 100 workouts for body armor, 50 stunt jumps for endless nitrous, and 25 bronze shooting-range medals for an ammo refill. Some, like the $250,000 cash drop, unlock just by finishing the story.

How do you enter a cheat?

Press up on the D-pad to pull out the phone, hold L1 and press R1 (LB and RB on Xbox), then put in the code. If a cheat still shows a lock, the code won't do anything.

What's the catch?

Two of them. Using a cheat stops you from saving and blocks trophies and achievements for that session. And starting a new save locks everything again, because unlock progress doesn't carry over. That's a big change from GTA V, where codes worked in your first hour.
