How Do You Fix the GTA 6 Chapter 3 Softlock? | Peekhole

November 26, 2026

Short answer: There is no official fix yet. The most reported workaround is to load a manual save from before the last Missing Persons meeting, play Jason's Dealer's Choice mission first, then make a fresh save and finish Missing Persons.

What is the bug?

Per Polygon, some players finish the Chapter 3 Missing Persons mission and find the switch from Lucia to Jason greyed out for good. The next story beat needs Jason, so the story stalls. Fan site GTA6Bugs says it is the most-viewed bug on its tracker.

Workarounds players report

  1. Load a manual save from before you drive to the Port Gellhorn warehouse for the final Missing Persons meeting.
  2. Switch to Jason and play Dealer's Choice until you can switch to Lucia freely.
  3. Save in a new slot, then finish Missing Persons.

Other tricks some players say worked: playing another Lucia or duo mission if one is available, calling Dunk from Lucia's phone, or robbing a store as a pair before starting Raul's job as Lucia. Not everyone has had luck, so keep backup saves.

Did update 01.002.002 fix it?

Apparently not. Polygon says the November 23 patch didn't seem to solve it. We'll update this when Rockstar ships a fix.
