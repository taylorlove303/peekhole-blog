GTA 6 launch week

Can You Get GTA 6 Free on Twitch or Kick? | Peekhole

November 16, 2026

Short answer: Yes, but only if you get lucky. Twitch and Kick are both giving away free GTA 6 Ultimate Edition codes around the November 19 launch. Nothing is guaranteed, and neither site says you need to pay to enter.

How the Twitch giveaway works

At TwitchCon on November 13, Twitch CEO Dan Clancy said the site will surprise-drop GTA 6 Ultimate Edition copies every day from launch through the end of November. Twitch has not said how many codes there are or exactly how you win one. The best bet is to be signed in and watching GTA 6 streams.

Separately, Twitch Drops run November 18 to 30. Watch a Drops-enabled GTA 6 stream for 60 minutes a day to collect chain badges, according to GameSpot. These are chat badges and emotes, not in-game items.

How the Kick giveaway works

Kick's "Sunset Reward Case" opens after you watch any stream for three hours in a day. Most cases hold emotes or badges, but GamingBible reports 10,000 Ultimate Edition codes are in the prize pool, for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and you can try again every day. TotalGTA notes codes won't be handed out before November 19.

Watch out for fakes

Only trust a prize that shows up inside your real Twitch or Kick account. A stranger in your DMs asking for a login or a "small shipping fee" is not Santa.
