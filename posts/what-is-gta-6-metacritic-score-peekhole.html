What Is GTA 6's Metacritic Score? | Peekhole

November 18, 2026

Short answer: 99. When the review embargo lifted on November 18 at 11:00 GMT (4 AM MST), GTA 6 landed a 99 Metascore on Metacritic. That ties The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (1998) for the highest score the site has ever shown.

How many reviews is that based on?

It moved fast. Make Tech Easier saw 99 from 100 reviews, then 99 from 109 about two minutes later. GTA Culture logged 99 from 115 by 11:25 GMT. Scores can still shift as more reviews arrive, so treat it as a snapshot.

What about OpenCritic?

Polygon reports a 98 on OpenCritic. For context, GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 both sit at 97 on Metacritic, so Rockstar just beat itself.

What are critics actually saying?

Game Informer and GameSpot both gave it a perfect 10. Reviewers keep praising the size of Leonida, the detail, and Jason and Lucia as leads. The complaints are small: Kotaku's reviewer mentioned some clunky controls and pacing issues late in the story, and compared them to "crappy cupholders in a million-dollar supercar."

What about the user score?

Still "tbd." Metacritic removed a wave of fake user ratings before launch, and real ones open once people can play.
