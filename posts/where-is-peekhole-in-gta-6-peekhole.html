Peekhole in the game

Where Is Peekhole in GTA 6? | Peekhole

November 22, 2026

Short answer: Peekhole shows up on a laptop in Boobie's Vice City apartment during Chapter 3. Unlock the laptop with the password MuffinsQueen15, then switch to the other browser tab. VGC describes Peekhole as the GTA 6 universe's adult content website.

How do you work out the password?

The game hides it in three clues around the apartment. A framed tribute to Boobie's late dog, Muffins, near the kitchen gives you the first word. A drawing by the bedside shows a dog wearing a crown, which is the "Queen" part. Flip that drawing over and the number 15 is on the back. Type it all together with no spaces.

What else is on the laptop?

Mostly Boobie's chat messages, which fill in his backstory. One message from an unknown sender is the one to read. It points to a secret garage in La Perle and gives the five-digit code 92665. Inside, according to VGC and Neon Bay, is a unique weapon tied to Boobie's ex. We won't spoil what it is.

Is it the same Peekhole from the poster?

It's the same in-game brand fans spotted before launch on the Jack of Hearts poster. The guides only describe a browser tab, so don't expect a full app you can scroll around in. Do this one in Chapter 3, since VGC warns not to leave it too late.
