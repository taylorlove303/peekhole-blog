What Phone Apps Are in GTA 6? | Peekhole

November 17, 2026

Short answer: Nobody has seen the full list yet. Rockstar's footage shows phone calls, text messages, livestreams, and a selfie-style camera. Other app names are reported but not confirmed in the game.

What has actually been shown?

According to GTA Boss's breakdown of official footage: a call screen with a timer and a red end-call button, text messages that pop up with the sender's picture, someone else's livestream with a live chat, and a camera viewfinder. Radio and podcasts can also play on foot through the character's phone and earbuds. The phone's home screen and app grid have not been shown.

What apps are reported?

  • WAINK: checks a car's security and trackers before you steal it.
  • Scooter Bros: brings your own car to you.
  • Ryde Me: a taxi app for getting around fast.
  • A banking app, a fitness app, and a social feed you can scroll.

All of these are reported, not confirmed in the game. Names like Snapmatic, Lifeinvader, and Bleeter show up on fan lists with nothing official behind them yet.

So is Peekhole an app?

Unconfirmed. Peekhole is a real name in official footage, printed under a QR code on a "Make It Rain Mondays" poster at the Jack of Hearts club. GTA6Intel says the code sends you to the official GTA VI website, and no source has shown subscribing, posting, or earning through it. Fans read it as an OnlyFans parody. Whether it lives on your in-game phone is something we will only learn after launch.
