How Do You Use RydeMe in GTA 6? | Peekhole

November 28, 2026

Short answer: Set a destination on the pause map, open the RydeMe app on your phone, and select Book Ryde. Wait for the car, hop in the back, and if you are in a hurry, hold L2 (LT on Xbox) and pick Skip Journey for $100.

How do you get the RydeMe app?

You do not have to hunt for it. The story adds RydeMe to your phone at the same point it adds the BuckMe bank app, and a little "Installed" notice pops up.

How do you book a ride?

  1. Drop a marker on the pause map.
  2. Open RydeMe and check the Trip Details screen: distance, ride type and price.
  3. Use Choose Ryde to switch between Standard Ryde and Premium Ryde if you like.
  4. Select Book Ryde and wait by the road. Do not wander off, or the driver cancels.

How much does RydeMe cost?

It depends on distance. AllThingsHow logged a 2.98-mile trip to The Rusty Anchor in Key Lento at $190, while a very short hop cost $18. Fares come out of your BuckMe account, so if you see "Insufficient funds," put cash in at an ATM first.

Why do players love it?

Leonida is huge, and fans on X joke that the back seat is the best seat in the game. Let someone else drive while you scroll your phone in style.
