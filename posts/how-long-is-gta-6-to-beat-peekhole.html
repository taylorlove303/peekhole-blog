How Long Is GTA 6 to Beat? | Peekhole

November 18, 2026

Short answer: Most reviewers reached the credits in about 60 to 80 hours. Rush the main missions and it can be closer to 40. Rockstar hasn't published an official length.

What did reviewers clock?

IGN's four writers finished in 42, 65, 70 and 115 hours, depending on how much they wandered. GameSpot says plan on 65+ hours for the campaign and 90 to 100 with side stuff. VGC says around 60 hours if you stick to the story. The Independent's reviewer beat it in 56.

How many missions are there?

GTA Culture, citing PC Gamer, says the story has 91 missions spread over five chapters.

What about 100% completion?

Way longer. GameSpot and VGC both estimate 200+ hours once you add every challenge, collectible and side activity. Reviewers who saw the credits were only about 60 to 72% complete.

Can I do a lot on launch day?

The Independent thinks you can finish the first two of five chapters in about 10 to 15 hours if you focus on the story. So yes, that day off is not wasted.

Did Rockstar ever give a number?

Only loosely. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told The New York Times his own run, the story plus some optional goals, took about 80 hours.
