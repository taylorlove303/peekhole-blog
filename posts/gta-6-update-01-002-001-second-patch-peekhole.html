GTA 6 launch prep

What Is GTA 6 Update 01.002.001? | Peekhole

November 16, 2026

Short answer: Update 01.002.001 is GTA 6's second update before launch. It is about 602.5MB on PS5 and about 1.6GB on Xbox Series X|S, it started rolling out on November 16, and Rockstar has not posted any patch notes.

Didn't I just get an update?

Probably. The first patch, 01.002.000, arrived on November 13 and was about 1GB. This one lands three days later, on top of it.

When will my PS5 get it?

VICE, citing dataminers, says Sony is sending it out in three waves 12 hours apart. About a third of PS5 players got it at 2 AM Pacific on November 16, two thirds at 2 PM Pacific, and everyone by 2 AM Pacific on Tuesday, November 17. That's 3 AM MST on the 17th. On Xbox it is already live.

What does it change?

Nobody outside Rockstar knows. ComicBook.com guesses small pre-launch patches like this could be security updates to stop leaks, but that's a guess, not a Rockstar statement.

What should I do?

Leave automatic updates on, keep your console in rest mode overnight, and save a little extra storage space. If you don't see it yet, wait for your wave. The game still unlocks at midnight local time on November 19.
