Is GTA 6 Too Easy? Rockstar Explains | Peekhole

November 25, 2026

Short answer: On purpose, a little. Rockstar says GTA 6's story missions are tuned to sit between GTA 4, which could be punishing, and GTA 5, which was too easy for some players. If you want a real fight, it points you to the open-world stuff instead.

What did Rockstar actually say?

Destructoid's Scott Duwe told Rockstar some story missions felt "on-rails." Head of development Rob Nelson answered by email that the team aimed "to land somewhere in the middle with GTA 6." The interview was published November 24, 2026.

Nelson also said designers tend to make missions too hard at first, because they know every enemy position by heart. A lot of the work is dialing that back so most players "enjoy, rather than endure."

Why do missions feel so forgiving?

Generous checkpoints and fewer fail states. Design producer Scott Mitchell said the team made "a conscious effort" not to overuse fail states, so you can lean on stealth and other tools without a mission ending the second you improvise.

Where's the challenge, then?

Per Nelson, some open-world content, like compounds and fronts, is deliberately tougher. So if the story feels like a gentle cruise down Ocean Drive, go pick a fight somewhere off the main road.

Is there a difficulty setting?

The interview doesn't mention one, and we haven't seen Rockstar confirm one. We'll update this if that changes.
