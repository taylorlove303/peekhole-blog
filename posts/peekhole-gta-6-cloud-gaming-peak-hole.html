Peekhole GTA 6 OnlyFans: Cloud Play for Everyone

November 11, 2026

Not every Peekhole regular upgraded the living-room hardware for GTA 6. Good news for the holdouts: you can still get a peak hole view of Vice City through the cloud.

Microsoft said Grand Theft Auto VI will be playable on day one through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, starting at 1:00 a.m. ET on November 19. Cloud streams will use Xbox Series S graphics settings, and the game has to be purchased separately. Series X and Series S owners can also pre-install and play natively; Xbox One players will be streaming.

The even more timely bit is today's date. Starting November 11, players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime to stream supported games without a Game Pass subscription. GTA 6 streaming itself still begins at 1:00 a.m. ET on launch day, and availability can vary by market and device. Microsoft also plans to add other Rockstar titles, including Grand Theft Auto V and Red Dead Redemption 2, to Xbox Cloud Gaming in the coming months.
