Who Plays Lucia in GTA 6? | Peekhole

November 24, 2026

Short answer: Lucia Caminos is played by Manni L. Perez. Her name became official on November 17, 2026, when she was nominated for Best Performance at The Game Awards, two days before GTA 6 launched.

Who is Manni L. Perez?

She's an actor with credits on Law & Order: Special Victims Unit and Jessica Jones, per GameSpot. TODAY describes her as a weightlifter and combat sports athlete, too, which explains why Lucia looks like she could bench-press Jason. GameSpot also notes she was already credited on GTA Online's Diamond Casino & Resort update, so fans had pegged her early.

Who plays Jason?

Dylan Rourke plays Jason Duval. Unlike Perez, he wasn't nominated, so his name only firmed up after launch. GameSpot lists small parts in Modern Family and Grey's Anatomy.

Any other famous voices?

A few. GameSpot's cast list has Stephen Root as Brian Heder, SNL alum Bobby Moynihan as Cal, Roselyn Sanchez as Mia Caminos, and Michael Jai White as Kordell Grant. Rapper Denzel Curry shows up too.

When will we know if she wins?

The Game Awards 2026 airs on December 10 from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles. GTA 6 has eight nominations in total, including Game of the Year, according to Deadline.
